# EX SPORTS™ SFC 27 STANDARD EDITION 1.0.0.4: player faces in SFC 27 ARENA

- Every SFC 27 ARENA player card now shows a head-and-shoulders portrait of that player in his club's kit colours:
  - the starting XI and bench from the team you picked
  - your reserves
  - every card from a pack.
- Each player has his own face, and it never changes. Faces vary in skin tone, face shape, hair style and colour, facial hair, eye colour and age (18 to 36).
- All players are fictional. No real footballers are depicted.
- Portraits are rendered in the game's own engine with plain lighting and no glow. They are saved for your user in `%APPDATA%\EX SPORTS SFC 27\faces`, so each one is made only once.
- New PLAYER INFO view: a large portrait with shirt number, position, age, rating, tier, club, squad place and stat bars.
  - On the ARENA hub or in a pack reveal, click a card to open it.
  - In SQUAD, switch on PLAYER INFO, then click any starter, sub or reserve.
- Everything else is the same as 1.0.0.3.
- The Setup is still per-user, with no admin rights.
