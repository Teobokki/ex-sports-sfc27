# EX SPORTS™ SFC 27 STANDARD EDITION 1.0.0.2: intro, SFC 27 ARENA, EX SPORTS RECORDER

## EX SPORTS intro
- A 6-second intro plays before the title screen on every launch. The EX SPORTS logo fades and scales in on black, holds, then fades into the title screen. There is no glow.
- It has an original sting and a British English voiceover: "EX SPORTS. IT'S IN THE FAME." The voice was rendered offline with Piper TTS (VCTK voice, CC BY 4.0).
- The intro follows the MASTER, SFX and the new VOICE volume settings.
- Press Esc, Enter or Space, or click, to skip it. Skipping stops the sound too.

## SFC 27 ARENA (new main-menu mode, next to KICK OFF)
- **PICK A TEAM** from the KICK OFF club list. Its name, kit and players become your starting squad of fictional player cards in Bronze, Silver, Gold and Elite tiers.
- **Squad**:
  - Formations 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2 and 5-3-2.
  - Click two players to swap them between the pitch, the bench and your reserves.
  - Set a captain and choose HOME, AWAY or YELLOW kits.
  - Team rating and chemistry are shown.
- **Packs** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Elite) are bought only with ARENA COINS, which you earn by playing. **ADD COINS** gives +1,000, +10,000 or +100,000 free. There is no real money and nothing to buy.
- **ARENA matches** are 11-a-side against an AI club and award coins at full time.
- **CHANGE TEAM** switches your base club and keeps your cards and coins. **NEW CLUB** starts over.
- Progress is saved locally in `%APPDATA%\EX SPORTS SFC 27\arena.json`.

## EX SPORTS RECORDER
- **F9** starts and stops recording. A small red REC indicator and timer appear on screen, but they are never in the video.
- **F10 SAVE MOMENT** saves the last 30 seconds.
- It works in KICK OFF, ARENA and ONLINE matches. The pause menu has a RECORDER entry, and the main menu has **RECORDINGS**, which lists your clips and opens Downloads.
- Clips save straight to your **Downloads** folder, e.g. `EX SPORTS SFC 27 - ARENA - 2026-10-22 22-19-05.mp4`.
- **YouTube-ready** MP4: H.264 High (yuv420p, constant frame rate, +faststart) with AAC 48 kHz stereo game audio.
- **QUALITY** (Settings): 720p30 (8 Mbps, default), 1080p30 (12 Mbps) or 1080p60 (18 Mbps).
- An optional small EX SPORTS watermark is off by default.
- It uses a bundled FFmpeg, so there's nothing to install and no admin needed. **Nothing is ever uploaded.**
