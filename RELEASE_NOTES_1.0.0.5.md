# EX SPORTS™ SFC 27 STANDARD EDITION 1.0.0.5: mixed squads in SFC 27 ARENA

- Every SFC 27 ARENA team now mixes men and women, roughly half and half:
  - the starter squad from the team you pick
  - the bench and your reserves
  - every pack card
  - AI opponents.
- Each player's gender is fixed by their player id, so it never changes.
- Women players have their own faces. These vary in skin tone, face shape, eye colour and age (18 to 36), with hairstyles such as ponytails, buns, braids, bobs, short cuts and curls. There is no facial hair.
- Women have female first names and a women's player model in ARENA matches. Every player in a match matches their card's skin tone and hair.
- Men keep their faces, names and player model.
- Existing ARENA saves are kept: every card's stats, rating, position, surname and squad place stay the same. Women's cards get a female first name.
- Portraits are re-rendered once for the new face version and cached in `%APPDATA%\EX SPORTS SFC 27\faces`.
- All players are fictional.
- KICK OFF is unchanged. There is no glow or bloom.
- The Setup is still per-user, with no admin rights.
