# EX SPORTS™ SFC 27 STANDARD EDITION 1.0.0

This is the first release.
- 7-a-side football in the EX SPORTS SFC 27 stadium: a full-size pitch, nets, stands with a crowd, and floodlights with no glow or bloom.
- **QUICK MATCH VS AI**, plus **ONLINE** play with an SFC27-XXXX-XXXX join code.
  - The AI fills the empty spots. You can play ALL OF US VS AI or SPLIT TEAMS. The host can kick players, and the lobby shows each player's ping.
  - Networking is outbound-first (STUN, UPnP, hole punching, REPLY CODE and keep-alives), with HMAC-signed packets and an optional password. It needs no admin rights and no firewall rule.
- **UNLIMITED** match length is the default. END MATCH shows the final score. Timed matches of 4, 8, 12 or 90 minutes are also available.
- **TEAMS** has fictional clubs, an all-yellow kit option and your own player look.
- **SETTINGS** covers match length, graphics, audio and controls. **CHECK FOR UPDATES** is in the menu.
- Per-user installer with no UAC prompt, plus a portable exe and a zip.
